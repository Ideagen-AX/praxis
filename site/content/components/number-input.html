Quantities, durations, counts, tolerances. In this domain most numeric fields also carry a unit, and the unit belongs in the control rather than in the label.

Anatomy

Not designed yet.

Variants

Plain; with stepper buttons; with a unit suffix ("mm", "ppm", "hours"). The suffix is the variant worth having and the one general systems most often omit.

States

Empty, filled, invalid, out of range, disabled. Out-of-range should say the bound — "must be 0 to 100" — not "invalid".

Markup contract

type="number" with inputmode="decimal", plus explicit min, max and step. Hide the native spinners and provide real buttons, or keep the native ones — but not both. Buttons need labels, and the unit suffix must be associated with the input, not floated beside it.

Accessibility

The unit must reach assistive technology, via the label or aria-describedby. Never rely on scroll-wheel increment. type="number" silently discards non-numeric input in some browsers, which is worth knowing before choosing it over a text input with validation.
