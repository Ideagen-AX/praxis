This is a promotion, not a new component. .admin-card already works and is used across the admin section and this reference site. What is missing is a neutral name for it. Tier 1 on ROADMAP.md; the aliasing approach is decision 2 there.

.card is on the gaps list today, and for a good reason: praxis-workspace.css gives it a slate shadow and a 12px radius and nothing else — no fill, no padding. So a .card is a shadow around transparent space. Meanwhile .admin-card is complete: --px-surface fill, --praxis-card shadow, 12px radius, 20px/22px padding, a __title, a __meta and a --flush density.

See Card, page and texture for what .card does today and why the page material matters underneath it.

Anatomy

  1. Surface — fill, radius, shadow. Present on .admin-card, absent on .card.
  2. Title — __title, 16px/700, 16px bottom margin.
  3. Meta — __meta, right-aligned count or timestamp in the title row.
  4. Body — unstyled. The card does not own its contents.

Variants

VariantState
DefaultExists as .admin-card
--flushExists. 16px/18px padding for a denser grid
Elevation tierNot exposed. praxis-core.css defines three tiers — --px-card-rail (recedes), --praxis-card (default), --px-card-raised (the subject) — with a comment explaining that a page of identically-shadowed cards reads as one flat plane. Only the middle one is reachable through a class. Candidates: --rail and --raised
InteractiveMissing. A card that is a link needs a hover and a focus ring. This reference site had to write both itself for its page grids

States

A static card has none. An interactive one needs hover, focus-visible and press — and the right hover depends on what the card sits on, which is the interesting part. On the page material a lift is correct; on another card a lift is wrong and the fill should step instead. This reference site solved that for itself and the reasoning is in site/assets/site.css under "THE SHEET"; the general answer belongs here.

Usage guidelines

Do

  • Use .admin-card today — it is the one that works.
  • Step to --px-surface-2 for anything inside a card rather than stacking shadows.
  • Let the grid size the card.

Don't

  • Use bare .card expecting a surface.
  • Give every card on a page the same elevation — that is what the three tiers exist to prevent, and the comment in praxis-core.css says so explicitly.
  • Put a card's padding on its contents. The card owns its inset.

Accessibility

  • A card is not a landmark. If it needs to be findable, its title is a heading.
  • An interactive card needs one clear target — a card wrapped in an anchor that also contains buttons produces nested interactive elements, which is invalid and unpredictable.
  • Focus-visible must be on the card, not only on a link inside it.
  • Forced colors: a card is a fill plus a shadow, and in forced-colors both the shadow and the fill distinction go. Every card on a page becomes one undifferentiated block. F2 again.
