Taken from GOV.UK, where it is a named component. A description field capped at 2,000 characters that only reveals the cap on rejection wastes the user's work — and in an incident report that work is a narrative someone typed once.

Anatomy

Not designed yet.

Variants

Count up to a limit, or count down remaining. Remaining is more useful. A word-count form exists in GOV.UK and is rarely what a regulated field wants.

States

Under limit (quiet), approaching (a threshold — GOV.UK uses the last 25%), over (danger, and the field is invalid). Whether input is truncated at the cap or allowed to exceed it is the open decision; allowing it and marking it invalid is kinder.

Markup contract

The message is associated with the textarea via aria-describedby and lives in a polite live region that is debounced — announcing on every keystroke makes the field unusable with a screen reader. GOV.UK debounces to roughly one second. maxlength alone is not enough because it gives no feedback.
