Distinct from a progress indicator: progress measures a task advancing, a meter measures a quantity within bounds. "94% of inspections completed this quarter" is a meter. Praxis has neither, and --praxis-elevation-era sheets contain no bar component at all.

Anatomy

Not designed yet.

Variants

Bar; a compact inline form for a table cell; and a banded form where the value falls into a named range (target / watch / breach). The banded form is the one this domain wants, and it is the one that must not rely on colour alone.

States

Within target, approaching a threshold, breached, and no data. "No data" is not zero and rendering it as an empty bar is a misstatement.

Markup contract

role="meter" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and aria-valuetext for the band name. The numeric value must also be visible as text; a bar alone is not readable to a precision anyone can act on.

Token reference

--praxis-color-status-success / -warning / -danger for bands, --px-chip for the track, --praxis-radius-full. No new tokens.

Accessibility

Value as text beside the bar. Bands differentiated by more than fill — a label or a pattern — under WCAG 1.4.1. role="meter" is not role="progressbar" and the two should not be conflated.
