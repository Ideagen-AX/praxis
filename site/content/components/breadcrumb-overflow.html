praxis-pageheader.css defines .breadcrumb properly, and its comment is explicit that "the breadcrumb is allowed to wrap at narrow widths and take the header with it" — height is a floor, not a fix. That is a deliberate choice, and it means a five-level path on a phone pushes the title down.

An asset hierarchy is routinely five or six levels. This page is about what to do then.

Anatomy

Not designed yet.

Variants

Collapse the middle into an overflow menu (Primer and Atlassian both do this); truncate individual labels with an ellipsis; or keep wrapping. Wrapping is the current behaviour and is defensible — the overflow menu hides the path, which for an asset tree is the information.

States

Full, collapsed, and expanded-from-collapsed. Where does focus go when the overflow opens is the detail that gets missed.

Responsive behavior

The whole point of the page. Desktop shows the full path; below some width it collapses or truncates. Which width, and which behaviour, is the open decision.

Markup contract

The overflow trigger is a button in the list, not a link. Collapsed items stay in the DOM so the path remains available to assistive technology and to in-page search — hiding them entirely would trade a layout problem for an information one.
