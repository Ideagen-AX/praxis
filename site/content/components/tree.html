Two things in Praxis sit next to this and neither is a tree. .rref is a reference-field picker and .admin-menulist is a flat menu list. A real hierarchy — Teesside → Blast furnace 2 → Interlock guard — has no representation.

The ARIA APG has both Tree View and Treegrid; the second is a tree whose rows carry columns, which is what an asset register actually is.
